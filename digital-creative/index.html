From market insight to product direction

Product research and design across mobility, automotive, and emerging technology markets

UI/UX Lead · Digital Creative
Client work across India, China, Brazil, and global automotive markets

Note: Client deliverables remain confidential. What follows is a public overview of the questions, methods, and kinds of outcomes — without proprietary research findings, internal metrics, or unpublished product details. Selected work available on request.

At Digital Creative I led product research and design work for clients navigating mobility, automotive, retail, and emerging technology markets. The through-line was translating field insight — interviews, comparative product study, cultural and market analysis — into directions teams could design and decide against.

IKEA — in-store experience research & AR navigation

Research into how customers move through the store — including self-checkout familiarity, queue behavior, and co-worker support — informed concept work for in-store AR navigation. Workshops and desirability studies backed a recommendation to pair a map-based navigating screen with an AR mode for aisle-level guidance.

Tata Group — EV insight research

Research into Chinese EV markets and innovation, translated into opportunities relevant to the Indian market. The work connected competitive and cultural signals abroad with product and strategy questions at home — what to learn from, what not to copy, and where local conditions demanded a different design response.

Volvo — navigation design research

Research exploring how drivers understand, use, and respond to navigation systems. Focused on real-world interaction: attention, trust, moments of confusion, and how interface decisions shape the experience of getting somewhere.

Lear — age-inclusive design research 适老化设计

Age-inclusive design research (适老设计) focused on making automotive experiences more accessible for older users. The work examined how aging, perception, and everyday use should inform product and interface decisions — not as an edge case, but as a core design constraint.

Fretebras — product research and design exploration

Product research and design exploration for Fretebras, examining a comparable “Uber for Trucks” model and what the company could learn from it. Delivered work spanning product design, market analysis, and expert interviews with company executives to understand decision-making, operating models, and transferable lessons.

The process moved through:

  • Clarifying what Fretebras needed to understand
  • Comparative study of a related product and operating model
  • Product design exploration grounded in that comparison
  • Market analysis
  • Expert interviews with executives on decisions, constraints, and transferable lessons
  • Recommendations the company could take forward

Role

UI/UX Lead responsible for research framing, qualitative and comparative methods, synthesis into product direction, and design exploration for client stakeholders.

Methods

Depth interviews · Expert interviews · Comparative product research · Cultural analysis · Usability testing · Journey mapping · Persona development · Prototyping · Interface design · Workshop facilitation
