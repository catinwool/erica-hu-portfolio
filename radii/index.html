Building a diasporic youth media platform

2020–2023
UX Research Lead
Assistant Product Developer

RADII is a media and community platform focused on the people, culture, and ideas shaping Asia and its global diaspora. I led 300+ hours of interviews to design and launch a new media platform, then helped redesign the website.

The work began during rising anti-Asian hate crimes and heightened US–China tension. We were building more than a publication. The goal was an active reading experience that could inform people, connect communities, and inspire action.

A diasporic youth audience wanted stories about identity, culture, politics, and belonging that did not flatten their communities the way mainstream media often did. Those findings became recommendations for editorial, product, and business development.

Redesigning the website

I later contributed to the redesign of RADII’s website, using research and behavioral data to evaluate the experience and prioritize what to change.

The redesigned platform contributed to approximately 10× growth in readership, from tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands.

The wider community reached a quarter million followers on Instagram, an important distribution network for its journalism and cultural work.

Methods

Ethnographic research · Depth interviews · Focus groups · Usability testing · Journey mapping · Persona development · Comparative product research · Interface design
