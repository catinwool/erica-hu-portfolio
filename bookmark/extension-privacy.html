Privacy

The Bookmark Chrome extension saves a page you choose to your private shelf at bookmark.erica-hu.com.

When you save, the extension reads the address and visible content of the active tab so a signed-in article can be kept in full. That material is sent only to your Bookmark account.

The extension does not sell data, show ads, or send page contents to any other service. You can remove the extension at any time from Chrome’s extensions page.

Questions: write to the email listed on erica-hu.com.
